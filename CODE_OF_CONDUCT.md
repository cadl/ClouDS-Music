# Code of conduct

ClouDS Music contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces
welcoming, technically constructive, and safe.

Be respectful of differing experience levels and backgrounds. Critique ideas
and code with concrete reasoning; do not harass, insult, threaten, publish
private information, or use sexualized or discriminatory language. Do not ask
others to share account credentials, copyrighted firmware, private media, or
personal data.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove
inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to `cadl@duck.com`. Include links or context
needed to understand the incident, but do not forward unrelated private data.
Reports will be reviewed as circumstances allow; confidentiality will be
protected to the extent practical.
